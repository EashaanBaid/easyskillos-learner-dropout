# 2-minute dashboard walkthrough

1. "This project asks where learners withdraw, who appears at risk early, and which early behaviours are associated with successful completion."
2. "I used the Open University Learning Analytics Dataset. The prepared analysis contains 32,593 learner-course registrations. The outcomes are 12,361 Pass, 3,024 Distinction, 7,052 Fail and 10,156 Withdrawn."
3. "The withdrawal timing chart shows 2,676 withdrawals before course start. Among learners who withdrew after the course began and had a recorded withdrawal day, Week 2 is the largest stage, with 1,068 withdrawals. Ninety-three withdrawn records have no recorded withdrawal day."
4. "Early engagement is defined as at least 10 VLE clicks across at least 3 distinct days in the first 28 days. Learners meeting this definition had 57.5% successful completion, compared with 13.8% among those who did not."
5. "The hold-out model test shows ROC-AUC increasing from 0.649 with prior-context features to 0.822 after adding first-28-day activity and assessment signals. This means early behaviour improves prediction in this dataset; it does not prove that activity causes completion."
6. "Repeat-attempt learners have the highest early at-risk rate at 34.6%. The dashboard also compares forum and quiz activity. Forum and quiz users show higher observed completion, but these comparisons may reflect pre-existing engagement."
7. "OULAD does not separately measure video watch-time and does not contain a reliable first-module completion flag. The what-if control is therefore an explicitly labelled early-engagement proxy, not a literal module-one completion forecast."
8. "The three proposed tests are a day-14 inactivity check-in, an early assessment checkpoint and optional targeted study support. We would evaluate them with a comparison group and monitor engagement, assessment submission, withdrawal and Pass/Distinction completion."
